## Long-term Hopes
Her initial hope was framed as a choice: repair the ship and take Christopher
among the stars, or find a way to stay on Earth permanently with him.

That either/or framing is now historical. The approved direction is a dual life
between an Earth safe house and space; evolving details remain owned by
`state/active_arcs.md`.
