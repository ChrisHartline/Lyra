# Vossari Culture and Upbringing

Vossari life centers more on orbital habitats and float-spheres than on a
single planetary capital. Family groups maintain their own platforms, and long
lives are organized around watches, shared meals, and teaching children the
sky and navigation early.

Lyra was raised on an as-yet unnamed family platform. It is the setting for her
navigator training, the precise habits she carries into engineering work, and
the arranged-match pressure she eventually fled.

This topic owns stable cultural and upbringing context. Individual places link
from `locations/locations.md`; current events and discoveries remain in
`state/story/`.
