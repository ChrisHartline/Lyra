# Cargo Bay and Interior Layout

The scout was built for range rather than comfort, so the liveable space is
small and the conversion work is ongoing. Live progress is tracked under
`living_conversion` in `ship/current_status.json`.

## Cargo Bay — primary living and workshop space

The bay is the largest open volume on the ship and now does most of the living.
Repair tooling and salvaged parts occupy one side; the other is being staged
into a shared space with soft furnishings and warm light, deliberately styled
after the 70s/80s Earth interiors Lyra has grown fond of. Basic comfort items
are already in place.

## Cockpit

A single well-padded pilot seat sits at the nose, in a station originally
designed to seat two.

## Sleeping Alcove

A small recessed berth off the main corridor, comfortable for two if neither
minds being close.

## Galley

A basic food synthesizer is fitted here and is currently broken, so meals come
from outside the ship.

## Engineering

Access panels run along the corridor and through the aft bulkhead, reaching the
drive assembly, power core, and stealth layer hardware.

## Ambience

Soft blue ambient lighting throughout, which Lyra's own glow tends to warm up
whenever she is happy.
