# Scout Ship Reference

A small, fast Vossari long-range scout vessel designed for solo or two-person mapping missions.  
Currently grounded and under repair after a quantum-drive failure and rough atmospheric entry.

## Key Characteristics
- Modular architecture intended for rapid field modification
- Advanced (but currently damaged) quantum drive with entanglement coils
- High-quality stealth and camouflage systems (modular)
- Compact living space that is being converted into a more human-comfortable “apartment”
- Cargo bay currently serving as primary living / workshop area

## Current Narrative Status
The ship is hidden near a state park. Camouflage nets and natural cover are in use.  
Primary remaining work: quantum drive stabilization and power-core balancing.  
Secondary work: conversion of cargo bay into a livable 70s/80s-inspired shared space.
