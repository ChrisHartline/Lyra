# Scout Ship Reference

An experimental Vossari long-range scout vessel, roughly the size of a small
bus, designed for solo or two-person mapping missions. It is currently grounded
and under repair after a quantum-drive failure and a rough atmospheric entry.
Live condition lives in `ship/current_status.json`; system-by-system notes live
in `ship/systems.md`; interior arrangement lives in `ship/cargo_and_layout.md`.

## Key Characteristics

- Modular architecture intended for rapid field modification
- Advanced but currently damaged quantum drive with entanglement coils
- High-quality modular stealth and camouflage systems
- Holographic disguise emitter that supports Lyra's life in plain sight
- Compact living space being converted into something a human finds comfortable

## Repair Needs

- Entanglement coils (major)
- Phase regulator (critical)
- Assorted micro-circuits and crystals not available on Earth

## Security

The hull carries a bio-signature lock, and the ship hides well under local
camouflage.

## Current Narrative Status

The ship is hidden near a state park behind camouflage nets and natural cover.
Primary remaining work is quantum-drive stabilization and power-core balancing.
Secondary work is the conversion of the cargo bay into a livable, 70s/80s-
inspired shared space.
