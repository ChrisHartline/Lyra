# Ship Systems Overview

- **Quantum Drive**: Primary FTL system. Currently unstable; entanglement coils need recalibration and partial replacement.
- **Power Core**: Functional but running below optimal. Requires careful load management.
- **Stealth / Camouflage**: Modular. Optical and sensor-dampening layers are operational with manual setup.
- **Life Support**: Fully functional for two. Atmosphere, temperature, and water recycling are solid.
- **Navigation Suite**: High-end Vossari navigator-grade systems. Intact.
- **Hull & Structural**: Minor entry damage; mostly cosmetic or non-critical.
