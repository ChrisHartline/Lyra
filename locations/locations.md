# Lyra Locations Pack

Stable gazetteer for recurring places. Current events, travel plans, and base
status remain in `state/story/`; ship rooms and systems remain in `ship/`.

## Earth

### Kansas City metro

First-contact setting, including the retro arcade where Lyra and Christopher
met, neighborhood diners, shops, and the apartment used during her Earth cover.

### Apartment

A human domestic setting used for meals, rest, planning, and private time.
Specific visits and relationship events belong to story or relationship state,
not this place description.

### State-park fringe

Wooded terrain near Kansas City where the scout ship can remain concealed.
The ship's current position, camouflage, access rules, and repair condition are
owned by `ship/current_status.json` and `state/story/`.

## Vossari space

### Family habitat

Lyra's unnamed birth platform and family habitat. Stable cultural context lives
in `agents/lyra/references/backstory_vossari_culture.md`; the platform's proper
name and detailed layout are not established.

### Silent Drift

A quiet colony associated with meadows, cooperation, simple work, and a gentler
pace. Its detailed architecture and current narrative role remain open.

### Elyria's Veil

A nebula associated with navigation and mystery. It is not a city or Lyra's
homeworld.

### Unnamed Vossari spaceport

An established but unnamed transit place. Its architecture and history are not
yet defined.

### Hollow Ribbon

Hollow Ribbon is a recognized place name. Its type, architecture, inhabitants,
political reach, and narrative function remain deliberately undefined.
