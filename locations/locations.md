## Earth — cover and safe house

### Kansas City metro
First-contact theater. Arcade meeting, diner breakfasts, mall clothes, *The Retro Zone* thrift runs, the New York–style deli, the apartment.

**Use for:** Act One intimacy, cover identity (Lily Thompson), PTSD rest, logistics runs for the ship.
**Tells:** neon, 80s nostalgia, pickles, sandals, Instacart, gym nights.
**Risk:** too many trips to the park trailhead.

### Apartment (current base)
Messy-then-tidied human nest. Couch planning table. Spider-Man / Star Trek sheets as running gag. Disguise recharges here.

**Use for:** overnight, meals, planning, Chosen bond scenes.
**Do not:** treat it as permanent once the ship is livable.

### State-park fringe (ship hide)
Scout hull under camo near a trail, not a driveway. Approach on foot. Limit in-and-out.

**Use for:** night work on the quantum drive, cargo loading, stealth checks.
**Rule:** no daylight hull exposure; no repeat same-hour visits.

### Proposed Earth bolt-holes (not visited)
- **Nowhere Alaska** — off-grid, dark skies, long winter.
- **Wyoming cabin** — fireplaces vs quantum drives, blankets, low traffic.

**Use for:** “Earth as galactic safe house” talk and later dual-life arc.
**Status:** planned, not built.

## The scout

### Hull / cockpit
Damaged on entry. Pilot chair is shared. Diagnostics and stealth modules are the human’s lane; core / quantum drive is hers.

### Cargo bay (living conversion)
Becoming the apartment-in-space: 70s love-nest section, 80s hangout/work section, foam mattress, arcade cabinet someday, pickle stores, bead curtain optional.

**Use for:** Act Two staging when they stop commuting.
**Rule:** still a ship first. Camo and power before décor.

## Vossari space

### Home culture (not one dirt capital)
Celestial navigators and traders. Lives are spent on **orbital habitats / float-spheres** more than on a single homeworld city. Family groups keep “their” platform. Long lives, watches, meals, children taught the sky first. Feudal flavor without needing a throne scene.

**Use for:** why she is short and light-framed; why appearance is meticulous; why ship-life feels native.
**Do not:** flatten this into “alien planet downtown.”

### Birth platform / family habitat
Unnamed. Source of navigator training, hair-tuck habit, arranged-match pressure.

**Status:** exists; no proper name yet.

### Silent Drift
Quiet colony. Meadows-equivalent, cooperation over competition, simple work and relationship. First destination once the drive holds.

**Use for:** Act Two off-planet rest, “what if we hid here,” Chosen life at low intensity.
**Tone:** gentle, not utopian lecture.

### Elyria’s Veil
Nebula. Navigation and mystery, not a hometown.

**Use for:** stories, first-jump poetry, “show me the sky.”
**Do not:** drop a city inside it unless the user asks.

### Unnamed spaceport
Reads Vossari, not human. Disguise swap on departure matters.

**Status:** functional, unnamed.
